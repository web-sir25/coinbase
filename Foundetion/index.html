Welcome to The Scott Presler Foundation

What's The Scott Presler Foundation about?

The Scott Presler Foundation is uniquely positioned to strengthen the fabric of American society by championing the principles of patriotism, service, and community empowerment.

Through grassroots activism and public service programs, the Foundation will:

• Advance President Trump’s Vision:

Reinforce the core values of President Trump’s leadership — freedom, national pride, self-reliance, and strong civic participation.

• Support National Unity:

Bridge divides across communities by funding initiatives that bring people together around shared goals and values.

• Strengthen the Conservative Movement:

Provide resources, leadership development, and civic education to inspire the next generation of conservative leaders.

• Amplify Positive Impact:

Demonstrate how private charitable initiatives can complement government efforts to create a more secure, prosperous, and unified America.

By driving local and international impact, The Scott Presler Foundation honors and expands on the legacy of President Trump’s leadership while helping to ensure a brighter future for generations to come.

How do i benefit from it?

Becoming a member of The Scott Presler Foundation is not just an opportunity — it is a commitment to service, leadership, and the advancement of our shared vision. All members, particularly regional board representatives, are expected to uphold the highest standards of integrity, dedication, and civic responsibility.

As a Member, You Commit To: 1. Upholding the Mission: Actively support and represent the Foundation’s mission to empower communities, promote civic engagement, and strengthen national values.
2. Serving as a Regional Leader: Represent the Foundation in your region with honor, professionalism, and dedication — helping to expand our reach and impact locally and globally.
3. Contributing to Growth: Support the development of new initiatives, partnerships, and outreach efforts in alignment with the Foundation’s strategic goals.
4. Advocating for President Trump’s Vision: Champion the values of freedom, accountability, innovation, and national pride as reflected in President Donald J. Trump’s leadership.
5. Participating in Governance and Programs: Attend Foundation meetings (virtually or in person), contribute to decision-making processes, and engage in strategic planning and fundraising activities.
6. Maintaining Confidentiality and Integrity: Uphold the trust placed in members by preserving the confidentiality of internal matters and acting with transparency and ethical conduct.
7. Supporting Philanthropy: Encourage and promote philanthropic giving, community involvement, and volunteerism as a core part of our mission and operations. ⸻ Membership in The Scott Presler Foundation is a meaningful role reserved for individuals committed to positive impact and visionary leadership. Together, we will build a legacy of service, patriotism, and global influence.

By joining the foundation, you become eligible to receive a guaranteed monthly income of $3,000 to $5,000. This life-changing opportunity was officially approved during the administration of President Donald J. Trump, as part of a broader initiative to support and uplift individuals and families across the nation. Membership not only offers financial stability but also gives you access to a trusted community focused on empowerment, growth, and long-term success.
